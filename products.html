ODYSSEY

Discipline. Precision. Consistency.

Odyssey is a fully automated trading robot dedicated to a single market: the German DAX 40 index. It does not spread itself across dozens of instruments or chase passing fads. It does one thing – and does it with discipline, every trading day.

One market, deeply understood

The DAX is one of Europe's most dynamic indices: fast moves, high volatility, daily opportunities. Odyssey was built exclusively for this market and tuned to its rhythm. Specialisation is its edge.

Trading without emotions

Fear, greed, haste or the urge to win back a loss are a trader's biggest traps. Odyssey has none of them. It executes every decision by clear, consistent rules, from the first trade of the day to the last.

Capital protection comes first

Every position has its risk defined from the moment of entry. Position size is calculated automatically, gains are protected along the way and losses are capped.

Always alert, never tired

Odyssey watches the market without a break for the whole session and reacts in real time, without delay. You get on with your day; it gets on with the job.

Tested in real conditions

Odyssey is not a lab experiment. It runs on a live account, in real market conditions, and is continuously evaluated and improved based on its results.

The Odyssey Advantage
One market. One robot. Zero emotions.

Odyssey does not promise guaranteed profits – no market offers them. Its mission is to bring the discipline, speed and consistency of an automated system to where most traders lose because of emotions.

Monthly Trend Portfolio

DAILY MARKET PORTFOLIO

Patience. Balance. Transparency.

The Daily Market Portfolio is a medium-term investment strategy, run by fixed rules and rebuilt once a month. It does not react to every headline and is not carried away by daily noise. It looks for the market's strong directions and follows them patiently.

Built on trends, not noise

Markets go through long periods of rising and falling. The portfolio identifies the markets with the clearest, most stable direction and builds its positions only there.

Diversified across asset classes

Stock indices, gold, silver, oil, currencies and technology stocks. The portfolio can profit from rises and falls alike – it does not depend on a single market or direction.

Risk, carefully spread

No position dominates the portfolio. Volatile markets get a smaller weight, calmer ones a larger weight. Every position has a protective level checked every hour.

Renewed every month

At the start of each month the portfolio is fully re-assessed: strong positions stay, those that lost strength leave, and new opportunities take their place.

Full transparency

Public from day one. Every entry, every exit and every result – win or loss – is visible on dailymarketlab.com. Nothing is hidden and nothing is rewritten after the fact.

The Portfolio Advantage
Less turbulence. More direction. Results in plain sight.

The Daily Market Portfolio does not promise guaranteed returns. Its mission is to show, month after month and in the open, what a disciplined strategy built on market trends – not emotions – looks like.
