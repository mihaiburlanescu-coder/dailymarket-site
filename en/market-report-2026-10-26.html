How the verdict is formed. BUY or SELL when the technical trend and options positioning point in the same direction, or when only one of them has a direction (STRONG when both agree or the trend is very strong); HOLD when they contradict each other or neither has a direction. It is a signal, not a probability: tested over 2007–2026, no model called the next day's direction better than chance. The ranges, on the other hand, are verified: the 70% range contained the close on 68%–73% of days (2019–2026).

Good to know about the VIX
  • The VIX shows how large a move the market expects in the SP500 over the next 30 days. It usually rises when stocks fall and drifts lower slowly when the market is calm.
  • VIX calls are bought mainly as protection for stock portfolios: buying calls is a bet that fear will rise. That is why a low put/call ratio (0.2–0.4) is normal for the VIX and does not mean optimism.
  • VIX options are priced off VIX futures, not off the index shown here, and they expire on a Wednesday (30 days before the monthly SP500 options expiry). At expiry they settle at a special VIX value calculated on the morning of that day.
  • The VIX tends to revert towards its long-term average (around 19–20). Spikes above 30 happen in moments of panic and usually do not last long.
  • VIX CFDs and ETFs track the futures, not the index. In calm periods the futures trade above the VIX and VIX products gradually lose value (contango), even if the VIX itself stays flat.

What you see on the chart

Candles – one candle = one hour. Green: it closed higher than it opened; red: lower. The wick shows the high and low of the hour. Hover over a candle to see the prices.
R1 – resistance 1: the first level above, where rallies often stall.
S1 – support 1: the first level below, where declines often stall.
Timeframe: one-hour chart (H1). The signal describes the short-term trend, i.e. the last 1–3 days, so it can change from one day to the next. R1 and S1 are calculated from the previous day's high, low and close and apply to the day shown at the top.
BUY/SELL describes the current trend; it does not guarantee that it will continue. The signal is not a statistically validated prediction.
