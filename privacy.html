Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What data we collect

  • Email subscription: your email address and the time you gave your consent.
  • Site account (if you create one): your name, age, email address and password, stored encrypted.

We do not use advertising cookies. The site does not show ads.

Visit statistics: we count visits without cookies and without storing your IP address. Each visit is linked to an anonymous code that changes every day, so we cannot recognise you from one day to the next. We record only the country or time zone, the type of device, the page and the report tabs opened, and the website you came from.

Google Analytics: if you click “Accept” in the cookie notice, we also use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) to measure, in aggregate, how many people visit and which pages they read. It sets analytics cookies (_ga) and IP addresses are anonymised. If you click “Decline” or make no choice, Google Analytics sets no cookies. Advertising features are switched off. You can change your choice at any time: cookie settings.

Why we use it

Solely to send you the Daily Market report by email and, if you have an account, to give you access to the site. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 – GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or share it for commercial purposes. We only use providers that help us operate:

  • Supabase – database and accounts, hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt);
  • Brevo – sending the emails;
  • GitHub Pages – hosting the site's pages.

Contact

For any question about your data, or to unsubscribe or delete your account, write to contact@dailymarketlab.com.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your account. After that, the data is deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and to withdraw your consent. If you are not satisfied, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country or with the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro.
